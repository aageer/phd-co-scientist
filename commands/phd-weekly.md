description: Weekly PhD operating cadence — SOTA delta, one experiment, one page of notes
---

1. `python -m swarm sota --year-min 2025` and note anything new.
2. One tournament (`ideate`) or one program mutation — not both unless small.
3. File logs under `runs/`.
4. Half-page lab note: what was verified, what failed, next metric.
5. No paper draft unless logs changed.
